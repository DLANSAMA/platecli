# T-1 · Rebuild the TUI's look, keep its engine

Chosen 2026-11-06 ("Let's go ahead and make those upgrades!"). Branch `feat/tui-redesign`.

## Keep
`tui/services.py` (status fetch, prepare pipeline, monitor service), `tui/deps.py`, `tui/app.py` wiring, the confirm flow and every safety rule, `interactive/core.py`. Views stay free of domain logic: anything that decides goes in a pure, tested helper in `services.py`.

## Change
- **Theme:** a `platecli` theme (teal primary, from the social card) plus a light twin, registered in `tui/theme.py` and made the default.
- **Top bar:** one line replacing Textual's `Header` on every screen: `plate`, the screen name, the printer (model · IP, or "simulated printer"), and a state badge on the dashboard.
- **State names:** `Printing`/`Paused`/`Finished`/`Failed`/`Idle`/`Preparing`/`Offline`, each with a tone. No raw `RUNNING` on screen.
- **Dashboard:**
  - A job hero: name, big percent (`Digits`), time left plus clock ETA, layer, which tray is feeding, and a full-width bar.
  - Temperature cards for nozzle and bed, with sparkline history (the screen keeps a ring buffer of snapshots).
  - The AMS as tray cards, each with a swatch in the filament's real colour, its material, and a remaining meter. Slots are named A1–A4 / B1–B4 like Bambu Studio, with an external spool card.
  - 80×24 fits without scrolling.
- **Monitor:** same hero widget; the "leaving never stops the print" hint stays.
- **Prepare/confirm/help/settings:** top bar, compact buttons so 80×24 fits, values that never wrap mid-unit, no duplicated titles.
- **Slot labels:** `interactive/core.ams_slot_label` moves to A1-style names, so `plate go`'s Feed row matches the TUI.

## Not in scope
A plate-thumbnail image (needs an optional image dependency and terminal graphics support): this goes in the inbox if it isn't done.
